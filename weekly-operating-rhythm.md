# Novela - Weekly Operating Rhythm

## The Philosophy
Themed days reduce context-switching and create predictable rhythms. Each day has a primary focus, with recurring tasks slotted into logical moments.

---

## Weekly Overview

| Day | Theme | Core Focus |
|-----|-------|------------|
| **Monday** | Strategy & Planning | High-level thinking, investor prep, week setup |
| **Tuesday** | Sales & Outreach | Prospecting, HubSpot, new business |
| **Wednesday** | Customers & Product | Customer check-ins, classes, content review |
| **Thursday** | Marketing & Comms | LinkedIn, newsletter, messaging strategy |
| **Friday** | Admin & Reflection | Finance admin, spending review, week wrap-up |

---

## Daily Breakdown

### Monday: Strategy & Planning
*Start the week with clarity on what matters*

- [ ] Review high-level strategic objectives - what's the #1 priority this week?
- [ ] Scan for newsworthy items (industry news, competitor moves, market shifts)
- [ ] Add to investor update draft (keep a running doc)
- [ ] Quick HubSpot triage - flag urgent follow-ups for Tuesday
- [ ] Set 3 "must-win" outcomes for the week

**Time block:** 2-3 hours morning for deep strategic thinking


### Tuesday: Sales & Outreach
*Revenue-generating activities get dedicated focus*

- [ ] HubSpot deep dive - who needs follow-up? Update pipeline
- [ ] Sales outreach - find and contact new prospects (aim for X new conversations)
- [ ] Respond to any warm leads from Monday
- [ ] Update CRM notes from any calls/meetings

**Time block:** Morning for outreach (higher response rates), afternoon for follow-ups


### Wednesday: Customers & Product
*Existing customers are your best salespeople*

- [ ] Customer check-ins - which classes are coming up?
- [ ] Prep/support for upcoming customer sessions
- [ ] Content project work - review and update materials for:
  - [ ] Professors
  - [ ] Students
- [ ] Collect any testimonials or success stories for marketing

**Time block:** Flexible - align with customer availability


### Thursday: Marketing & Comms
*Build the brand and pipeline for future*

**LinkedIn (batch create for the week):**
- [ ] Personal post #1
- [ ] Personal post #2
- [ ] Personal post #3
- [ ] Novela post #1
- [ ] Novela post #2

**Content themes to draw from:**
- Latest news/trends in AI + Marketing Education
- Novela traction and milestones
- Customer wins and insights
- Founder POV on industry

**Other marketing:**
- [ ] Newsletter prep (biweekly - AI + Marketing Education)
- [ ] Marketing messaging strategy work (project deadline: [END OF NEXT WEEK])

**Time block:** Morning for creative work, schedule posts throughout the week


### Friday: Admin & Reflection
*Clear the decks, close the week*

**Finance admin:**
- [ ] Process Gmail receipts → Drive folder → FreeAgent
- [ ] Check Revolut - review spending, flag anything unusual
- [ ] Any VAT prep needed?

**Week wrap-up:**
- [ ] What worked this week? What didn't?
- [ ] Update investor news doc with week's highlights
- [ ] Quick wins to celebrate?
- [ ] Prep Monday's strategic review

**Time block:** 1-2 hours, ideally early afternoon to end week on a clear note

## Tips for Making This Work

1. **Protect the theme** - If someone wants a sales call on Friday, push to Tuesday
2. **Batch the small stuff** - Receipts and admin pile up; Friday clears them
3. **LinkedIn hack** - Write all 5 posts Thursday, schedule throughout the week
4. **Strategic thinking needs space** - Monday morning should be meeting-free
5. **Be flexible** - Customer emergencies and hot leads trump the theme
