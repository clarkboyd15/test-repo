# VA Delegation Guide for Novela

## Where to Find a VA

### UK-Based VAs (Higher cost, same timezone, native English)
| Platform | Cost | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| [Time Etc](https://web.timeetc.co.uk) | £27-35/hr | UK-based, vetted, good for exec support |
| [Virtalent](https://virtalent.com) | £30-40/hr | UK, university-educated VAs |
| [AVirtual](https://avirtual.co.uk) | £35-45/hr | Premium UK service |

### International VAs (Lower cost, may need timezone management)
| Platform | Cost | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| [Belay](https://belaysolutions.com) | $25-35/hr | US-based, highly vetted |
| [Athena](https://athenago.com) | $3k/mo full-time | Philippines, trained for founders |
| [Time Etc (US)](https://timeetc.com) | $35-55/hr | US-based |
| [OnlineJobs.ph](https://onlinejobs.ph) | $5-15/hr | Direct hire from Philippines, you manage |
| [Upwork](https://upwork.com) | $10-40/hr | Freelance marketplace, variable quality |
| [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com) | Variable | Good for one-off tasks, less for ongoing |

### Recommendations for Your Situation

**If budget is tight:** Start with OnlineJobs.ph or Upwork. Hire for 5-10 hrs/week. You'll need to train them but cost is low.

**If time is tighter than money:** Use Time Etc or Athena. Pre-vetted, less management overhead.

**Start small:** 5 hours/week (~£75-150/week UK, ~$50-75/week Philippines). Scale up as you build trust.

## What to Delegate (Your VA Task List)

### Tier 1: Delegate Immediately (Low risk, high time savings)

#### Finance Admin
- [ ] Process receipts from Gmail → Drive folder
- [ ] Upload receipts to FreeAgent
- [ ] Categorise transactions in FreeAgent
- [ ] Chase unpaid invoices (using your template)
- [ ] Prepare VAT return documentation
- [ ] Reconcile Revolut with FreeAgent monthly

#### Email Management
- [ ] Unsubscribe from junk
- [ ] Sort inbox into folders (Urgent / This Week / FYI / Archive)
- [ ] Draft responses to routine emails (you approve before sending)
- [ ] Flag emails that need your personal response

#### Calendar Management
- [ ] Schedule meetings (using your booking link or back-and-forth)
- [ ] Send calendar reminders for prep needed
- [ ] Block focus time in your calendar
- [ ] Reschedule conflicts

#### Research
- [ ] Find prospect contact details for sales outreach
- [ ] Research companies before sales calls
- [ ] Compile industry news for investor updates
- [ ] Find relevant conferences/events

### Tier 2: Delegate With Training (Medium complexity)

#### CRM / HubSpot
- [ ] Update contact records after your calls
- [ ] Log activities you report verbally
- [ ] Generate weekly pipeline reports
- [ ] Flag stale deals (no activity > X days)
- [ ] Clean up duplicate contacts

#### Content Support
- [ ] Schedule LinkedIn posts (you write, they post)
- [ ] Repurpose content (turn blog → LinkedIn carousel)
- [ ] Source images/graphics for posts
- [ ] Track engagement metrics weekly
- [ ] Manage newsletter subscriber list

#### Customer Admin
- [ ] Send class reminder emails
- [ ] Collect feedback post-class
- [ ] Update customer records
- [ ] Prepare customer meeting briefs

### Tier 3: Delegate Once Trust is Built

#### Sales Support
- [ ] Send initial outreach emails (using your templates)
- [ ] Follow up with cold prospects
- [ ] Book qualified meetings in your calendar

#### Marketing
- [ ] First draft of newsletter
- [ ] Social media engagement (likes, comments on target accounts)
- [ ] Maintain content calendar

## How to Brief Your VA

### The Initial Onboarding Doc

Create a shared doc (Notion/Google Doc) with:

```markdown
# Working With [Your Name]

## About Novela
[2-3 sentences on what you do, who you serve]

## My Communication Style
- I prefer [Slack/Email/WhatsApp] for quick questions
- Response time expectation: [within X hours]
- For urgent issues: [call/text me]

## Working Hours
- Your hours: [flexible / set times]
- My hours: [when you're available]
- Timezone: [yours]

## Tools You'll Need Access To
- Gmail (delegate access)
- Google Drive
- FreeAgent (limited access)
- HubSpot
- [Calendar tool]
- [Slack/communication tool]

## Weekly Check-in
- Every [Friday] we'll have a 15-min call/async update
- You'll report: tasks completed, blockers, questions
- I'll provide: priorities for next week
```

### The Task Brief Template

For each new task type, create a brief:

```markdown
# Task: [Name]

## What
[One sentence description]

## Why
[Why this matters - helps them make judgment calls]

## Steps
1. [Step one]
2. [Step two]
3. [Step three]

## Tools
- [Tool 1] - login details in [password manager/doc]

## Quality Check
- Before marking complete, verify:
  - [ ] [Criteria 1]
  - [ ] [Criteria 2]

## Examples
- Good: [link to example]
- Bad: [what to avoid]

## Questions?
- If unsure, ask me before proceeding
- Common edge cases:
  - If [X], then [Y]
  - If [Z], then ask me

## Time Estimate
[X minutes per item / X hours per week]
```

## Example Brief: Receipt Processing

```markdown
# Task: Weekly Receipt Processing

## What
Find receipts in Gmail, save to Drive, upload to FreeAgent.

## Why
Keeps us VAT-ready and saves me 1-2 hours per week of admin.

## Steps
1. Search Gmail for: receipts, invoices, payment confirmations
   - Search: "receipt OR invoice OR payment" newer_than:7d

2. For each receipt:
   - Download the PDF/image
   - Rename: YYYY-MM-DD_VendorName_Amount.pdf
   - Save to: Google Drive > Novela > Finance > Receipts > [Year] > [Month]

3. Upload to FreeAgent:
   - Go to Banking > Receipt Bank (or Explain tab)
   - Upload the receipt
   - Match to transaction if visible
   - Categorise using the category guide below

4. Mark email as processed (apply "Processed" label in Gmail)

## Category Guide for FreeAgent
- Software subscriptions → "Software & IT"
- Travel → "Travel & Transport"
- Client meals → "Entertainment"
- Office supplies → "Office Costs"
- If unsure → Flag for me to review

## Time Estimate
30-60 minutes per week

## Done When
- All receipts from past week saved to Drive
- All receipts uploaded to FreeAgent
- Gmail search shows no unprocessed receipts from past 7 days
```

## VA Onboarding Checklist

### Before They Start
- [ ] Create their email/tool accounts
- [ ] Set up password sharing (1Password, LastPass, or shared doc)
- [ ] Write initial task briefs for first 3 tasks
- [ ] Prepare onboarding doc (above)
- [ ] Schedule intro call (30 mins)

### Week 1
- [ ] Intro call: explain Novela, your working style, expectations
- [ ] Assign ONE task only (e.g., receipt processing)
- [ ] Daily check-in (5 mins async or call)
- [ ] Give feedback on first outputs immediately

### Week 2
- [ ] Add second task if first is going well
- [ ] Reduce check-ins to every other day
- [ ] Document any SOPs they helped improve

### Week 3-4
- [ ] Add remaining Tier 1 tasks
- [ ] Move to weekly check-ins
- [ ] Start building Tier 2 tasks if ready

### Ongoing
- [ ] Weekly 15-min sync
- [ ] Monthly review: what's working, what's not
- [ ] Quarterly: expand scope or adjust hours

## Red Flags to Watch For

- Missed deadlines without communication
- Repeated mistakes on the same thing
- Goes silent when stuck (instead of asking)
- Quality drops over time
- Doesn't follow SOPs after training

**If you see these:** Direct feedback first. If no improvement after 2 weeks, consider replacing.

---

## What NOT to Delegate (Keep These)

- Final approval on anything customer-facing
- Strategic decisions
- Investor communications (they can prep, you send)
- Sales calls (they can book, you close)
- Content that's your personal voice (they can draft, you edit)
- Anything requiring your passwords to sensitive financial accounts
