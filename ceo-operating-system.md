# CEO Operating System - Anti-Drift Edition

## The Core Problem
You need to be across everything, but your brain wants to go deep on one thing. This system creates **external forcing functions** so you don't have to rely on willpower to surface.

---

## 1. The Daily Cockpit (5 mins, non-negotiable)

A single-page view you check every morning. Not a rabbithole - a **scan**.

### Build a dashboard with these numbers:
| Area | Metric | Source | Target |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| Cash | Runway (months) | Revolut/FreeAgent | >X months |
| Sales | Pipeline value | HubSpot | £X |
| Sales | Deals closing this week | HubSpot | X |
| Customers | Classes scheduled next 7 days | Your system | X |
| Marketing | LinkedIn impressions (week) | LinkedIn | Trending up |
| Marketing | Newsletter subscribers | Your ESP | X |

**The rule:** Look at the dashboard. If a number is red, that's today's priority. If all green, execute the themed day.

**Tools:** Notion dashboard, Google Sheet, or a simple checklist you update manually. Start manual - automate later.

---

## 2. The Rabbithole Protocol

When you notice you've gone down a hole:

### The 3-Question Exit Ramp
1. **"Is this my #1 priority today?"** (Check your daily 3)
2. **"Will this matter in a week?"**
3. **"Am I the only person who can do this?"**

If any answer is "no" → stop, capture it, return to plan.

### Capture, Don't Complete
- Keep a "Parking Lot" note open at all times
- When something interesting pulls you: write 1 line, return to task
- Review parking lot on Friday - most items will seem less urgent

### Hard Boundaries
- **Timer:** 25-minute blocks (Pomodoro). When it rings, you surface.
- **Physical interrupt:** Stand up, walk to a different room, then decide if you continue
- **Scheduled exploration:** Give yourself ONE 90-min "rabbithole block" per week (Thursday afternoon?). This legitimises the instinct.

---

## 3. The CEO Checklist (Daily, <2 mins)

Before you start work, complete this:

```
□ What are my 3 must-dos today? (Write them on paper, visible)
□ Checked the dashboard?
□ Any fires? (Slack/email scan - 5 min MAX, not a reply session)
□ Calendar reviewed - do I know what's happening today?
□ Phone on Do Not Disturb until first block done?
```

End of day:
```
□ Did I complete my 3 must-dos?
□ If not, why? (Drift? Emergency? Wrong priorities?)
□ What's tomorrow's #1 priority?
□ Anything to add to parking lot?
```

---

## 4. The Weekly CEO Review (Friday, 30 mins)

This is your "be across everything" ritual. Block it. Protect it.

### The Review Checklist
```
MONEY
□ Checked Revolut transactions
□ Runway still healthy?
□ Any unexpected costs?

SALES
□ HubSpot pipeline reviewed
□ Who needs follow-up next week?
□ Any deals stuck? Why?

CUSTOMERS
□ Which customers did we serve this week?
□ Any at-risk relationships?
□ What's scheduled next week?

MARKETING
□ Did we hit our content targets? (LinkedIn, newsletter)
□ What performed well?
□ Marketing messaging project - on track?

STRATEGY
□ Are we still working on the right thing?
□ Anything to add to investor update?
□ What did I learn this week?

OPERATIONS
□ Receipts processed?
□ Any admin piling up?
□ Team (if applicable) - anyone need my attention?
```

**Output:** A short bullet list of "Next week's priorities" - max 5 items.

## 5. Anti-Drift Forcing Functions

### External Accountability
- **Weekly co-founder/advisor call:** 30 mins, same time each week. "What did you commit to? Did you do it?"
- **Body doubling:** Work on video call with someone else working. The social presence prevents drift.
- **Public commitment:** Post your weekly priorities somewhere (private Slack, accountability partner). Review against them Friday.

### Environment Design
- **One tab rule:** Close everything except what you're working on
- **Phone in another room** during deep work
- **Visible priorities:** Write your 3 daily must-dos on paper, put it next to your screen
- **Themed days:** You already have this - the theme is the forcing function

### Time Boundaries
- **Hard start, hard stop:** Calendar blocks have end times. When it ends, you stop.
- **Meeting with yourself:** Block focus time as meetings. Treat them as unmovable.
- **Transition rituals:** Between tasks, stand up, take 10 steps, then start the next thing.

## 6. The "Across Everything" Cheat

You can't hold everything in your head. Don't try.

### Weekly Information Architecture
| Day | What you're naturally across |
|-----|------------------------------|
| Monday | Strategy, priorities, investor narrative |
| Tuesday | Sales pipeline, prospects, HubSpot |
| Wednesday | Customers, product, content |
| Thursday | Marketing, brand, messaging |
| Friday | Finances, operations, week summary |

**The cheat:** On each themed day, you're the expert on that domain. The weekly review on Friday synthesises everything. You're not "across everything all the time" - you're across the right thing at the right time, with a weekly sync.

### The 15-Minute Monday Briefing (to yourself)
Start each week by writing (or voice-noting) answers to:
1. What's the #1 thing that matters this week?
2. What could derail us?
3. What am I avoiding?
4. What decision have I been postponing?

This surfaces the important stuff your subconscious knows but hasn't told you.

## 7. When You Notice Drift

### In the moment:
1. Stop
2. Say out loud: "I'm drifting"
3. Write down where you are in the rabbithole (so you can return if it's actually important)
4. Check your 3 daily priorities
5. Set a 25-min timer and start the most important one

### Pattern recognition:
- Track when drift happens (time of day, task type, energy level)
- Common triggers: after meetings, when tired, when task is ambiguous
- Design around it: Hard tasks in the morning. Clear next actions. No ambiguity.

## 8. Tool Stack (Keep It Simple)

| Need | Tool | Rule |
|------|------|------|
| Dashboard | Notion/Sheet | Check once daily, morning |
| Task capture | Todoist/Things/Apple Notes | Capture only - review weekly |
| Parking lot | Single note, always open | Write, don't act |
| Timer | Physical timer or app | 25-min blocks |
| Calendar | Google/Outlook | If it's not blocked, it doesn't exist |
| Focus | Cold Turkey/Freedom | Block distracting sites during work blocks |

---

## The Meta-Rule

**You don't need more willpower. You need better systems.**

Your instinct to follow interesting things is a strength - it's why you can go deep on problems others give up on. The job of these systems is to **channel that energy**, not suppress it.

The goal isn't to become someone else. It's to build scaffolding that lets you be effective *as you are*.
